Godforged Phase 31 — Material & Architecture Pass

Built from the actual Phase 30 ZIP. Gameplay, controls, item shop, lanes, tower zones, and abilities are unchanged. Adds seven locally generated 512px textured material maps (wood, plaster, slate, masonry, iron, bark, foliage), uses them on houses, trees, towers and minions, and adds visual details to towers and minions. These are improved procedural materials, not professional photorealistic models or skeletal animations. Browser and iPhone performance remain untested.
