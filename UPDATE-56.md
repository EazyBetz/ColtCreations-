# Sunken Empire — update 56

Matches start at level 1 and end at level 30. Earn XP from combat rewards and 5 XP per living second. Each level gained awards one skill point; levels and builds reset in a new match. Account progression and cosmetic rewards remain separate and unimplemented.

Open SHOP to spend skill points on Might, Vitality, Resolve, or Mastery (up to 10 ranks each). Spend gold in your sanctuary on six distinct equipment paths with three tiers costing 200, 400, and 700 gold. AI gods earn XP and automatically invest in power, health, and cooldown ranks. Co-op players have separate host-validated levels, purchases, and skill points.

The map adds eroded bank meshes, shoreline foam, animated water geometry, statues, broken columns, rubble, jars, and vegetation. Character hip/knee and shoulder/elbow timing is differentiated. Titans now use articulated Roman armor and proportions. Spell effects add ground ripples and secondary sparks.

Verification: gameplay VM, progression boundary tests, profile-flow tests with mock API, and live deployed source comparison. Actual phone rendering and two-device network sessions require device testing.
